## Decisions for you

1. **The update renewal price** of UGX 300,000 a year was my choice. Change it in Super if you want another figure.
2. **Have the data protection pack and the six guides checked** by a lawyer and someone who knows the current rules before clients rely on them. The Act's section numbers, the 72-hour breach deadline and the fees in the guides were written without the official texts to hand.
3. **Refunds after insurance payments.** Any payment can be refunded to the patient, including insurance money. Only admins and accountants can approve one. Say if insurance money should be kept out.
4. **Stock valuation.** When an item's cost changes, the shelf value and the books can drift apart. Weighted-average cost would fix this, but it changes how every item is valued, so I have not switched it.
