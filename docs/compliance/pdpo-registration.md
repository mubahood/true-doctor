# PDPO registration checklist

The Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (section 29) requires every organisation that collects or processes personal data to register with the **Personal Data Protection Office** (PDPO), part of the National Information Technology Authority, Uganda (NITA-U). {clinic} collects patients' health data, so it must register. Registration is renewed every year.

## Before you start, have ready

- [ ] The clinic's legal name, registration or licence number, physical address, phone and email.
- [ ] The name and contacts of the person in charge of data protection at the clinic (the data protection officer, if you have appointed one).
- [ ] A description of the personal data you hold: patients' identity, contacts and health records; staff records; billing and insurance records.
- [ ] Why you hold it: providing health care, billing, insurance claims and reports to the Ministry of Health.
- [ ] Who you share it with: insurers (for claims), the Ministry of Health (counts only), and the company that runs your records system ({processor}, for the online service).
- [ ] Whether any data leaves Uganda, and where (for example, email or text message providers used by the system). Ask us if you are not sure: {processor_email}.
- [ ] How you keep it secure: summarise the controls in the data protection impact assessment.
- [ ] How long you keep it: the retention schedule.
- [ ] The registration fee for your category (check the current fee with the PDPO).

## Steps

1. [ ] Create an account on the PDPO registration portal (pdpo.go.ug) and fill in the form with the details above.
2. [ ] Pay the fee and keep the receipt.
3. [ ] Keep the certificate and its number. Put the number on your privacy notice.
4. [ ] Put a reminder in the calendar to renew before it expires.

## After registering

- [ ] Print the privacy notice for patients and put it up in the waiting room.
- [ ] Accept the data processing agreement (online service): Settings, Data protection.
- [ ] Name who handles data requests and breaches, and walk them through both procedures.
