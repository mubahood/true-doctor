## What happens at the end

When a record reaches the end of its period, the clinic's administrator reviews it, then it is deleted or made anonymous (the name and contact details removed, the clinical facts kept only as numbers for reports). A record involved in a complaint, a claim or a legal case is kept until that is settled.

## Who decides

The clinic's administrator owns this schedule. Review it once a year, and whenever the Ministry of Health, the Medical and Dental Practitioners Council or the tax authority changes its guidance. The periods above come from common practice and the laws named; confirm them with your own adviser.
