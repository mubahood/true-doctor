# Getting started

This chapter shows you how to sign in to True-Doctor, how to protect your account with two-step sign-in, and how to find your way around the screen. It ends with a table of the staff roles and what each one usually sees.

If your hospital has just started using True-Doctor, the Hospital Admin should read [Setting up your hospital](02-setting-up.md) next.

## Signing in

You sign in with your work email address and your password. Your Hospital Admin creates your account, and True-Doctor emails you a temporary password.

1. Open your hospital's True-Doctor address in a web browser. The **Sign in** page opens.
2. Type your work email in **Email address**.
3. Type your password in **Password**. Click the eye icon if you want to see what you typed.
4. Leave **Keep me signed in on this device** ticked on your own work computer. You stay signed in for 90 days. Untick it on a shared or public computer, so you are signed out when the browser closes.
5. Click **Sign in**.

If you get the email or password wrong several times, True-Doctor shows a **Quick check: type the characters in the picture** box. Type the characters you see and try again. After too many wrong tries, True-Doctor makes you wait a short while before you can try again.

An account that has been switched off cannot sign in, even with the right password. Ask your Hospital Admin to switch it back on.

### Your first sign-in

The first time you sign in with the temporary password, True-Doctor asks you to choose your own. The **Set your password** page opens and you cannot go anywhere else until you finish it.

1. In **Password you signed in with**, type the temporary password from the email.
2. In **New password**, type your own password. It must have at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers.
3. Type it again in **Type the new password again**.
4. Click **Set password & continue**.

If this is not your account, click **Sign out** at the bottom of the page.

### If you forget your password

1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Type your work email and answer the quick check.
3. Click **Email me a reset link**.
4. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then ask your Hospital Admin. They can set a new password for you from **User accounts**, and you will be asked to change it when you next sign in.

If you still cannot get in, click **Trouble signing in? WhatsApp us** on the **Sign in** page.

## Two-step sign-in

Two-step sign-in adds a second check after your password: a 6-digit code from an app on your phone. A stolen password alone then cannot open your account.

It is optional. Anyone can turn it on for themselves from **Account security**, and it is a good idea for owners and anyone who handles money or patient records. If someone loses their phone, a Hospital Admin opens their account in **User accounts** and clicks **Turn off two-step sign-in**; they then sign in with the password alone.

### Turning it on

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy.
2. In True-Doctor, click your name at the top right, then **Account security**.
3. Under **Two-step sign-in**, click **Set up two-step sign-in**.
4. In the authenticator app, add an account and scan the square code on the screen. If you cannot scan it, type the key shown under **Cannot scan? Type this key instead**.
5. Type the 6-digit code the app shows, then click **Turn on two-step sign-in**.
6. True-Doctor shows your **recovery codes**. Each one works once if you lose your phone. Print them or write them down and keep them somewhere safe. They are shown only this once.
7. Click **I have saved them. Continue**.

### Signing in with two-step sign-in

After your email and password, the **Enter your code** page opens.

1. Open the authenticator app and type the 6-digit code it shows for True-Doctor in **Code**.
2. On your own work computer, tick **Trust this browser for 30 days** so you are not asked every morning. Untick it on a shared computer.
3. Click **Continue**.

If you lost your phone, type one of your recovery codes in **Code** instead. If you have no phone and no recovery code, click **No phone and no recovery code? WhatsApp us**.

### Other things on the Account security page

- **Make new recovery codes**: type your password and click this if you have used most of your codes. The old ones stop working.
- **Turn off**, or **Move to a new phone** for Hospital Admins: type your password and click it, then set up two-step sign-in again on the new phone.
- **Trusted browsers**: click **Ask every browser again** if you trusted a computer you no longer use.
- **Apps signed in**: shows each phone signed in to your account with the mobile app. Click **Sign out** beside one you do not recognise.
- **Sign out everywhere else**: type your password and click this if you lost a phone or signed in on a computer you do not trust. You stay signed in where you are.

## Finding your way around

Every page has the same frame: a menu on the left, a bar across the top, and the page itself in the middle.

### The menu on the left

The menu is grouped under three headings: **PATIENT CARE**, **BILLING & FINANCE** and **ADMINISTRATION**. Click a group, such as **Billing**, to open it and see its pages. A group with only one page for you shows as a single link.

You only see the pages your role allows. Some pages also disappear when your hospital does not use that part of the system. For example, a dental clinic that does not admit patients has no **Inpatient** group, **Wards** or **Beds**. Your Hospital Admin chooses this under **Your practice** (see [Setting up your hospital](02-setting-up.md)).

At the top of the menu is your hospital's name and logo. Click it at any time to go back to the dashboard.

At the bottom of the menu is **Sign out**.

On a phone or small screen, the menu is hidden. Click the button with three lines at the top left to open it, and the cross to close it.

### The bar across the top

From left to right:

- The **menu button** (three lines) and the **name of the page** you are on.
- The **Help and support** button, a question mark. It opens a short list:
  - **Guide for this screen** (or **Guides**) opens a written guide about the page you are on.
  - **WhatsApp support** starts a WhatsApp message to support with the name of your screen already filled in.
  - **Call** and **Email support** show the support phone number and email.
- The **Notifications** bell. A number on the bell shows how many notifications you have not read.
- **Your name**. Click it for:
  - **My profile**: change your name, phone, a short bio and your photo, then click **Save**.
  - **Change password**: type your **Current password**, your **New password** twice, then click **Update password**.
  - **Account security**: two-step sign-in and where you are signed in (see above).
  - **Sign out**.

### Notifications

True-Doctor sends you notifications about work that concerns you. For example, a doctor is told when a lab result they ordered is ready, a pharmacist is told when a stock item falls to its reorder level, and a doctor is reminded of their upcoming appointments.

1. Click the bell at the top. The **Notifications** page opens with the newest first.
2. Unread notifications have a **New** label. Click **Mark read** beside one, or **Mark all read** at the top.

The number on the bell updates by itself about once a minute.

### The bottom of the page

The footer shows your hospital's name, your own name and role, today's date and the time. It is a quick way to check which account you are signed in with.

### When the internet drops

If your computer loses its connection, a bar appears at the bottom of the screen to say you are offline and that changes cannot be saved until the connection returns. Wait for it to disappear before you save. Staff who must keep working with no signal should use the mobile app, which is built for that. See [The mobile app](14-mobile-app.md).

### Asking before you confirm

When you do something that cannot easily be undone, such as archiving a department or deleting a user, True-Doctor asks you to confirm in a small box first. Read it, then click the button to go ahead or cancel.

## The dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see after you sign in. It is different for each role: it shows the numbers you watch and the work waiting for you, with **Quick actions** at the bottom for the pages you use most. The figures refresh by themselves about once a minute while the page is open.

| Role | What the dashboard shows |
|---|---|
| Hospital Admin | Key figures (**Patients today** and the treatments done, open visits, **Appointments today** and the next 7 days, **Collected today** and **Collected this month** by the cashier's own day, **Outstanding**, **Low stock**, and **Not billed this month**: products used without billing, at cost), **Visits in progress** (how many visits are **Not started**, **In care**, at **Billing** and at **Payment**, and how many are held up by an open order), money billed against money collected, bed occupancy, **Work outstanding**, revenue by payment method, cards and insurers, today's appointments by status, and low stock |
| Doctor | **My open visits** and **Today's diary** |
| Nurse | **Waiting for vitals** and **Medication round**, the doses due |
| Receptionist | **Today's appointments** and **Awaiting payment** |
| Pharmacist | **Low stock**, **Expiring soon** and **Top dispensed today** |
| Lab Technician | The lab **Worklist**, **Orders by stage** and recent abnormal results |
| Radiologist | The **Report queue**: studies performed and waiting for a report |
| Accountant | Revenue for the last 14 days, revenue by method, **Invoices by status**, **Outstanding invoices** and, where the hospital bills insurers, **Pending insurance claims** |
| Records Officer | **Patients by status** and **Recently registered** |

Most boxes on the dashboard are links. Click a figure, such as the number of visits at **Payment**, to open the list behind it.

A box only appears when it applies to your hospital. For example, bed occupancy only shows when your hospital admits patients.

While the hospital is still being set up, the Hospital Admin sees the **Set up your hospital** page instead of the dashboard. See [Setting up your hospital](02-setting-up.md).

## Roles: who does what

Every account has one role. The role decides what you can see and do. Your Hospital Admin gives you your role when they create your account.

| Role | What they usually do | Main menus they see |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital Admin | Runs the hospital in True-Doctor: staff accounts, settings, prices, and can do any clinical, billing, pharmacy, lab or finance task. Approves refunds | Everything, including the **Settings** group, **User accounts** and **Audit log** |
| Doctor | Sees patients, opens and works on visits, records diagnoses, prescribes, orders lab and imaging, looks after admitted patients. Puts charges on the bills of their own patients | **Visits**, **Scheduling**, **Lab orders**, **Radiology orders**, **Inpatient**, **Diagnoses to code**, **Patients** |
| Nurse | Takes vitals, gives medicines, records treatments, looks after admitted patients | **Visits**, **Scheduling**, **Inpatient**, **Patients**, **Immunisations due** where the hospital offers it |
| Receptionist | Registers patients, books appointments, checks patients in, opens visits, takes payments, gives discounts, issues cards, calls patients due for a review | **Visits**, **Scheduling** (with **Recalls and reviews due**), **Patients**, **Billing**, **Cards**, **Insurance claims** |
| Pharmacist | Dispenses medicines, manages stock, suppliers and orders | **Visits**, **Pharmacy** (stock, alerts, ledger, purchase orders, suppliers), **Stock categories**, **Patients** |
| Lab Technician | Processes lab orders, enters results, keeps the lab test list | **Visits**, **Lab orders**, **Lab tests**, **Patients** |
| Radiologist | Carries out imaging and writes reports, keeps the imaging study list | **Visits**, **Radiology orders**, **Radiology studies**, **Patients** |
| Accountant | Prices, invoices, refunds, insurance claims, the books and reports | **Billing**, **Cards**, **Insurance claims**, **Finance**, **Reports**, **Ministry reports (HMIS)**, **Price list** |
| Records Officer | Registers and keeps patient records, codes diagnoses for the Ministry reports | **Patients**, **Patient links**, **Diagnoses to code** |

Every role can also open **Departments**, **Rooms**, **Staff profiles**, **Price list** and **Offline readiness** under **ADMINISTRATION**, but only the people with the right role can change them.

A menu also depends on what your hospital uses. If your hospital has no laboratory, nobody sees **Lab orders**, whatever their role.

### What each role does not see

Clinical notes are for the clinical team. The Receptionist and the Accountant see that a visit happened, its reason and its bill, but not the complaints, the diagnosis, the doctor's notes, the prescriptions, the dental chart, the results or the visit report. Where a page has one of these parts, they see **You do not have access to this. Ask your hospital admin.** in its place. The Receptionist still sees the vitals of a visit, because the desk often takes them at check-in; the Accountant does not.

A Doctor sees the bills of their own patients on the visit, but not the clinic's money: the invoice list, the **Cashier's day**, the takings and the reports. A Nurse records vitals and gives treatment but cannot change the diagnosis.

### Your clinic can change this

The table above is the standard. Your Hospital Admin can change what each role may do in **Settings**, **Staff and access**, and can give one person an extra ability on their account (for example the senior nurse who also takes payments). See [Staff and access](02-setting-up.md#staff-and-access).

If you need a page you cannot see, you see **You do not have access to this. Ask your hospital admin.** Ask your Hospital Admin. They can change your role on the **User accounts** page, or what your role may do on the **Staff and access** page.
