# Mother and child health

This chapter is for midwives, nurses, doctors and clinicians who run antenatal and child clinics. True-Doctor keeps three records for mothers and children: the pregnancy record with its antenatal contacts, the immunisation card, and the growth chart. Each is a tab on the patient record. There is also one list, **Immunisations due**, that shows which children need a dose today.

These records follow the national schedules: the eight antenatal contacts of the WHO model as used in the Uganda Clinical Guidelines, the national (UNEPI) immunisation schedule, and the WHO Child Growth Standards. Always check against the current Ministry of Health cards in your clinic.

## When these tabs appear

The tabs appear only where they make sense, so a busy patient record does not fill up with empty tabs.

| Tab | Shows for |
|---|---|
| **Pregnancy** | A woman aged 12 to 55 with a date of birth recorded, in a practice that includes maternity |
| **Immunisation** | A child under 15, in a practice that includes paediatrics or maternity |
| **Growth** | A child under five, in a practice that includes paediatrics or maternity |

A tab also appears for any patient who already has something recorded on it, whatever their age or your practice type. The **Immunisations due** menu entry shows for practices that include paediatrics or maternity. A dental clinic, for example, sees none of these. Your Hospital Admin chooses the specialties under **Your practice** (see [Setting up](02-setting-up.md)).

### Who can do what

- **See** the tabs and the due list: anyone who can see visits, such as Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, Radiologist and Hospital Admin.
- **Record** pregnancies, contacts, doses and measurements: Nurse, Doctor and Hospital Admin. The midwife at triage normally signs in as a Nurse.

The date of birth and sex matter a great deal here. Without them the schedules cannot be worked out. If they are missing, ask the front desk to correct the patient's details (see [Patients](03-patients.md)).

## Pregnancy

### Recording a pregnancy

1. Open the patient and choose the **Pregnancy** tab.
2. Press **Record a pregnancy**.
3. Enter the **Last menstrual period**. True-Doctor works out the **Expected delivery** date (280 days later). If a scan gives a different date, change **Expected delivery** to the scan date.
4. Enter **Gravida** (pregnancies, this one included) and **Para** (births before this pregnancy). Para must be less than gravida.
5. Tick any **Risk factors** that apply, such as Previous caesarean, High blood pressure, Diabetes, HIV positive, Anaemia or Twins or more. Add **Risk notes** if needed.
6. Fill in the **Birth plan**: **Place of birth**, **Companion**, **Transport**, **Blood donor** and **Plan notes**.
7. Press **Save**.

A woman can have only one current pregnancy at a time.

### What the tab shows

Across the top you see the **Gestation** today and the trimester, the **Expected delivery** date and whether it came from the LMP or was set by the clinician, **Gravida / para**, and the **Next contact** with its week and date.

If there are risks, a **Risks** box lists them. As well as the risk factors you ticked, True-Doctor adds some by itself: under 18, over 35, five or more births, a blood pressure of 140/90 or more at the last contact, and past 41 weeks.

**Due at this contact** is a checklist of what should be done at the next contact, for example blood pressure, weight, fundal height, urine protein, haemoglobin, HIV and syphilis tests, Td, iron and folic acid, IPTp, deworming and a mosquito net. A check stays on the list until it is done. A check that has a deadline, such as the ultrasound scan before 24 weeks, moves to **Missed** once that time has passed.

**Antenatal contacts** shows the eight contacts at 12, 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40 weeks, with the due date and status of each: Upcoming, Due, Missed or Done. A woman who books late shows her early contacts as missed. That is correct: it tells you what she did not receive.

### Recording an antenatal contact

1. On the **Pregnancy** tab, press **Record contact**.
2. Check **Seen on** (today by default).
3. Enter **Blood pressure** (for example 120/80), **Weight (kg)**, **Fundal height (cm)** and **Fetal heart rate**.
4. Under **Done at this contact**, tick each check you did. The checks due at this contact are highlighted.
5. Add **Notes** and press **Record**.

The contact is placed against the right contact number by the gestation on the day she was seen.

### Editing and closing

Press **Edit** on the **Pregnancy record** card to correct the dates, gravida, para, risks or birth plan. Every change is logged.

When the pregnancy ends, press **Close**, choose the **Outcome** (**Delivered**, or **Ended otherwise (loss, transfer)**), the date in **On**, any **Notes**, and press **Close pregnancy**. Closing is final. The pregnancy then moves to **Earlier pregnancies** with its outcome and number of contacts.

## Immunisation

### The immunisation card

Open the child's record and choose the **Immunisation** tab. At the top you see how many doses were **Given**, how many are **Due now**, how many are **Overdue**, and the **Next** dose with its date.

The **Immunisation card** lists every dose of the national schedule by age: BCG and OPV 0 at birth, then polio, DPT-HepB-Hib, pneumococcal and rotavirus doses at 6, 10 and 14 weeks, IPV at 14 weeks, measles-rubella and yellow fever at 9 months, measles-rubella again at 18 months, and HPV for girls at ten years.

Each dose shows a status:

- **Upcoming**: not due yet.
- **Due**: from its due date for two weeks.
- **Overdue**: more than two weeks past its due date.
- **Missed**: past the last age the dose can be given (for example OPV 0 after two weeks, rotavirus after two years). It is no longer chased.
- **Given**: with the date, the batch number, and "elsewhere" if it was given at another facility.

A dose can fall due later than its age when the previous dose in the series was given late, because doses need a minimum gap between them.

### Recording a dose

1. On the **Immunisation** tab, press **Give** on the dose's row.
2. Check **Given on**.
3. Enter the **Batch number** from the vial.
4. If you are copying a dose from the child health card that was given at another facility, tick **Given elsewhere (copied from the child health card)**.
5. Press **Record**.

True-Doctor refuses a dose that is not on this child's schedule, already given, dated in the future or before birth, given more than four days too young, or given too soon after the previous dose. Read the message and check the card.

### Correcting a dose recorded by mistake

Doses are never deleted. Press the cross on the given dose, enter a **Reason** and press **Void**. The entry stays in the register with your reason, and the dose can be recorded again correctly.

### Immunisations due

**Immunisations due** in the menu lists every child with a dose due or overdue on a given day, overdue first. For each child you see the age, phone number and the doses due with their dates.

1. Use the search box to find a child by name or number.
2. Choose **Due and overdue**, **Due** or **Overdue** to filter.
3. Change the date to see who will be due on another day, for example to plan an outreach session.
4. Click a child's name to open the **Immunisation** tab and record what was given.

The line above the list counts the children and the overdue doses. Use the phone numbers to call parents whose children are overdue.

## Growth

### The growth tab

Open a child's record and choose the **Growth** tab. Three figures sit at the top:

- **Weight-for-age**: the latest weight, its band and its z-score.
- **Length/height-for-age**: the latest length or height, its band and z-score.
- **MUAC**: the latest mid-upper arm circumference and its band. MUAC is read from 6 to 59 months: below 11.5 cm is severe, below 12.5 cm is moderate.

The **Weight-for-age** chart plots the child's weights against the WHO standards. The middle line is the median, the dashed lines are minus 2 and plus 2, and the lowest line is minus 3. Below minus 2 is underweight; below minus 3 is severe.

The **Measurements** table lists every measurement, newest first, with the age, weight, length or height, their z-scores, MUAC, and where it came from: **Child clinic** for measurements recorded on this tab, or **Visit vitals** for weights and heights taken as vitals on a visit. You do not have to weigh a child twice: a weight taken at triage appears here by itself.

### Recording a measurement

1. On the **Growth** tab, press **Record measurement**.
2. Check **Measured on**.
3. Enter **Weight (kg)**, **Length/height (cm)** (measure lying down under two years) and **MUAC (cm)**. Fill in what you measured; you can leave the others empty.
4. Tick **Bilateral pitting oedema** if present.
5. Add **Notes** and press **Record**.

### Warnings

A red box appears at the top of the tab when:

- **Growth faltering**: the child has gained no weight since a weighing at least four weeks earlier, or has dropped sharply against the standard since the previous weighing. The box says which.
- **Bilateral oedema** was recorded at the last measurement. Treat this as severe acute malnutrition.

If the chart says to record the date of birth and sex, the child's details are incomplete. Ask the front desk to fix them.
