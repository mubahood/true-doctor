# Setting up your hospital

Before your team can start work, the Hospital Admin tells True-Doctor about the hospital: what kind of practice it is, its name and address, its currency, its departments, its prices and its staff. This chapter walks you through the **Set up your hospital** wizard and then each settings page you can come back to later.

Most of this chapter is for the Hospital Admin. Other staff can read the setup pages but cannot change them, apart from the Accountant, who can also change the **Price list**.

## The Set up your hospital wizard

When a new hospital's Hospital Admin first signs in, True-Doctor opens **Set up your hospital**. Until the required steps are done:

- the Hospital Admin's menu shrinks to one **Configuration** group, holding the wizard and the pages its steps use. A short note above it says the rest of the menu unlocks when the hospital is set up.
- clicking the logo at the top of the menu brings you back to the wizard instead of the dashboard.

Other staff are not held in setup. They see their normal menu.

### How the wizard works

The page shows a progress bar, a **Still needed** list of steps that are not done yet, and a **Checklist** on the right. Each step opens into a small form. Every step explains why True-Doctor needs it.

1. Click a step to open it, or click its name under **Still needed**.
2. Fill in the form and save. The step shows a tick when it is done.
3. Click **Next** to move to the following step, or pick any other step.
4. When every required step is done, the heading changes to **Your hospital is ready**. Click **Go to my dashboard**.

Nothing in the wizard is final. You can change all of it later, from the wizard (**Settings**, then **Set up your hospital**) or from each step's own page in the menu. If something passes but still looks wrong, such as tax switched on at 0%, it appears under **Worth a second look**.

If you are stuck, click **Stuck? WhatsApp us** at the top of the wizard.

### The steps

| Step | Required | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Your kind of practice** | Yes | Choose what kind of practice you run. See [Your practice](#your-practice) below |
| **Hospital details** | Yes | Name, address, a phone number or email, time zone and logo |
| **Currency and billing** | Yes | Currency, symbol and invoice prefix |
| **Departments** | Yes | At least one active department |
| **Price list** | Yes | At least one active, priced service |
| **Your team** | Yes | At least one colleague besides you |
| **Consultation rooms** | No | The rooms where patients are seen |
| **Wards and beds** | No | Only shown if you admit patients |
| **Lab and pharmacy catalogues** | No | Lab tests and stock categories, for the parts you use |

Many steps offer a **suggested** list to start from: common departments, common services with prices, rooms, wards with beds and a daily rate, lab tests and stock categories. The suggestions follow your kind of practice.

1. Look through the suggested rows. Change any price or number you want.
2. Click the cross beside any row you do not need. The next suggestion takes its place.
3. Click the button to add the rest, for example **Add these 12 departments**.
4. To add one of your own, fill in the small form under the list and click, for example, **Add this department**.

Rows you have already added show in a table with a pencil to edit and a bin to remove.

In the **Your team** step, type the person's **Full name**, **Work email**, **Phone** and **Role**, then click **Send the invitation**. True-Doctor emails them a temporary password.

## Your practice

**Settings**, then **Your practice**. This is the same form as the first wizard step.

What kind of practice you are decides what True-Doctor suggests everywhere: services and prices, departments, rooms, wards, lab tests, stock, the words offered when a clinician types a diagnosis, and which parts of the system appear in the menu. Changing it never removes anything you already have.

1. Under **Which is closest to yours?**, click the card that fits best: for example **General hospital**, **Medical clinic / health centre**, **Dental clinic**, **Eye clinic & optical**, **Maternity & women's health**, **Children's clinic**, **Diagnostic laboratory** or **Imaging centre**.
2. Under **What you offer**, the services that come with your choice are listed. Click any extra service you also offer, for example a clinic that also has a dental chair, or a maternity home with an ultrasound.
3. Under **Parts of the system you'll use**, switch on or off: **Inpatient care**, **Laboratory**, **Imaging**, **Pharmacy & stock**, **Insurance** and **Prepaid cards**. What you switch off is left out of everyone's menu. You can switch it back on at any time, and nothing is lost. Click **Use the suggested set** to go back to the usual choice for your practice.
4. Click **This is my practice** the first time, or **Save changes** later.

### Setting up the rest in one go

After you save, a box called **Set up the rest in one go** appears. It lists what your practice's starter set would add (departments, priced services, rooms, wards, lab tests, imaging studies, stock items, and accounts for the books) and how many of each you do not have yet.

1. Tick the parts you want.
2. Click **Set it up**.

Only what you do not have yet is added. Nothing you already have is changed, and every price stays editable. Stock items are added with an opening quantity, recorded as stock you already owned. Check the prices and quantities afterwards, because they are only a starting point.

## Hospital letterhead

**Settings**, then **Hospital letterhead**. What you enter here is printed at the top of every invoice, receipt, lab and radiology report, discharge summary and patient ID card. The right side of the page shows **How a document will look** as you type.

Under **Brand**:

- **Logo**: a square logo works best. It shows at the top of the menu, on the browser tab and on patient cards. PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB.
- **Wide logo (optional)**: a wide version with your name in it, for the top of printed documents and the sign-in page.
- **Brand colour**: buttons, links and the menu take this colour. Leave it empty to keep the standard blue.
- **Tagline (optional)**: a short line, such as your motto, for the sign-in page and emails.

Under **Details**: **Hospital name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Website**, **Licence or tax number**, **Address** and **Footer line**. The footer line is the last line of every document. It is the same setting as the footer under **Billing settings**.

Click **Save letterhead** when you are done.

## Departments

**Hospital**, then **Departments**. Appointments, visits and staff are organised by department. You need at least one.

1. Click **New department**.
2. Fill in **Name**, a short **Code** (for example DEN), a **Description** if you want one, and the **Head of department**.
3. Leave **Active** ticked.
4. Save.

To start from a list of common departments, click **Import samples**, choose the ones you want and click **Import departments**.

To stop using a department, click the archive icon on its row and confirm. Each name and code can only be used once in your hospital.

## Rooms

**Hospital**, then **Rooms**. Rooms let you book appointments into a real space and stop two clinicians being booked into the same one.

1. Click **New room**.
2. Fill in **Name**, choose a **Type** (**Consultation**, **Ward**, **Theatre**, **Laboratory**, **Radiology**, **Pharmacy**, **Office** or **Other**) and a **Department** if it belongs to one.
3. Set **Capacity**, the number of patients seen in it at once.
4. Set **Status**. Only a room that is available can be booked. Mark it as under maintenance or closed when it cannot be used.
5. Add **Notes** if needed, then save.

Wards and beds for admitted patients are set up under **Wards** and **Beds**. See [Inpatient care](09-inpatient.md).

## Staff accounts and roles

**Hospital**, then **User accounts**. Every person who uses True-Doctor needs their own account, so that work is recorded against the person who did it. Never share an account. Only the Hospital Admin can manage accounts.

### Adding a staff member

1. Click **New user**.
2. Fill in **Full name** and **Email**. The email is how they sign in, and each email can only be used once.
3. Choose a **Role**. The role decides what they can see and do. See the roles table in [Getting started](01-getting-started.md#roles-who-does-what).
4. Add a **Phone**, a short **Bio** and an **Avatar** photo if you want.
5. For someone who is not a doctor but also sees patients, for example a dentist who is also the Hospital Admin, tick **Also sees patients**. They can then be booked for appointments and given clinic hours while keeping their role.
6. Leave **Active** ticked.
7. Click **Create user**.

True-Doctor makes a temporary password and emails it to them. They choose their own password the first time they sign in.

On the hosted service, your subscription plan sets how many staff accounts you can have. If you reach the limit, True-Doctor tells you when you try to add another.

### Changing or resetting an account

1. Click the pencil on the person's row.
2. Change what you need. To change their role, pick a new **Role**.
3. To reset a forgotten password, type a new one in **New password** and **Confirm password**. Leave both blank to keep the current password. After a reset, they must choose a new password the next time they sign in.
4. To give this one person something more than their role allows, tick it under **Extra abilities for this person**. For example, tick **Take payments** for the senior nurse who also works the till. Only the Hospital Admin sees this part.
5. Click **Save changes**.

Only a Hospital Admin can make somebody else a Hospital Admin.

### When someone leaves

Edit their account and untick **Active**, then save. They can no longer sign in, but everything they did stays recorded under their name. This is usually better than deleting. You can delete an account with the bin icon, but you cannot delete your own.

True-Doctor will not let you remove, switch off or change the role of the last active Hospital Admin. Make somebody else a Hospital Admin first.

## Staff and access

**Settings**, then **Staff and access**. This page shows your clinic's roles against what each may do, in plain words. Only the Hospital Admin can change it. Anybody else who opens it can look but not change.

| Ability | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **See clinical notes** | Complaints, diagnoses, notes, prescriptions, the dental chart, lab and X-ray results, the visit report |
| **Record vitals** | Temperature, blood pressure, weight and the other observations |
| **Diagnose and prescribe** | Writing the clinical notes and the diagnosis, charting teeth, writing prescriptions |
| **Take payments** | Adding charges to a bill, raising the invoice and receiving money |
| **Give discounts** | Taking an amount or a percentage off a bill, with a reason |
| **Approve refunds** | Credit notes and money given back |
| **Change prices** | The price list |
| **See reports and money** | All invoices, the **Cashier's day**, takings, debtors, reports and the books |
| **Manage stock** | Stock items, receiving, adjusting and counting |
| **Manage staff and settings** | User accounts, staff profiles and the clinic's settings |

### Changing what a role may do

1. Find the ability in the left column and the role across the top.
2. Tick the box to give it to everybody in that role, or untick it to take it away. The change is saved at once and a message tells you what changed.
3. Some abilities go together. **Record vitals** and **Diagnose and prescribe** need **See clinical notes**: ticking one of them also ticks **See clinical notes**, and unticking **See clinical notes** also unticks them.

A box you have changed from the standard settings is marked. To put a role back to the standard settings, click **Standard** under its column.

The **Hospital Admin** column cannot be changed. The Hospital Admin always keeps every ability, so your clinic can never lock itself out of this page.

The change applies straight away on the computer and in the app. When True-Doctor is updated, your choices stay as you set them.

### Extra abilities for one person

The second part of the page lists the people who have something more than their role allows. You give or take these on the person's account in **User accounts**. See [Changing or resetting an account](#changing-or-resetting-an-account).

### Checking who changed what

Every change on this page, and every extra ability given or taken, is kept in the **Audit log** with who did it and when.

## Staff profiles

**Hospital**, then **Staff profiles**. A user account is how a person signs in. A staff profile holds their professional details. Each user can have one profile.

1. Click **New profile**.
2. Choose the **Staff member**. You cannot change this later.
3. Fill in **Department**, **Job title**, **Specialty**, **Licence no.** and **Qualifications**.
4. Leave **Active** ticked and save.

To stop using a profile, click the archive icon on its row and confirm.

Clinic hours for doctors are set under **Doctor availability**. See [Appointments and the queue](04-appointments-and-queue.md).

## Services and prices (Price list)

**Catalogues**, then **Price list**. Consultations, procedures and other work are billed from this list. A visit cannot be invoiced until something on it is priced. The Hospital Admin and the Accountant can change prices. Everyone else can look.

1. Click **New service**.
2. Fill in **Name** and a **Code** if you use one.
3. Choose a **Specialty**. It groups the price list, for example Dental.
4. Type the **Price**.
5. If the price varies, type the highest amount in **Up to (optional)**. For a range such as 50,000 to 80,000, the cashier can then charge any amount in between.
6. If you can see **Earns into**, leave it on **Automatic** unless your accountant tells you otherwise. It decides which income account the service is recorded under in the books.
7. Tick **Tax exempt** if no tax is charged on this service.
8. Leave **Active** ticked and save.

To start from a list of common services, click **Import samples**, adjust the prices, and click **Import services**.

To stop offering a service, click the archive icon on its row and confirm.

Lab tests, imaging studies and stock items have their own price lists. See [Lab and radiology](06-lab-and-radiology.md) and [Pharmacy and stock](08-pharmacy-and-stock.md).

## Billing settings

**Settings**, then **Billing settings**. This decides how money is written everywhere: on screen, on invoices and on receipts. The right side of the page shows **What this looks like**: an amount, your next invoice number, and a sample bill with tax.

Set the currency before you take your first payment. Changing it later changes how amounts are shown, but does not convert amounts already recorded.

### Currency

1. Click your currency under **Common**, for example UGX. This fills in the code, symbol and decimal places together.
2. Check **Currency code**, **Symbol**, **Symbol position** (**Before the amount** or **After the amount**) and **Decimal places**. Shillings usually use **0, no cents**.
3. Check the **Thousands separator** and **Decimal separator**.

### Tax

1. Tick **Charge tax on invoices** if you charge tax.
2. Fill in **What it is called**, for example VAT, and the **Rate (%)**.

Leave the box unticked if you do not charge tax. Single services can be marked **Tax exempt** on the price list.

### Invoices

- **Number prefix**: the letters at the start of every invoice number. With the prefix INV, invoices are numbered like INV-2026-00001.
- **Default visit fee**: charged when a visit is opened with nothing else on it. Leave it at 0 if you do not charge one.
- **Footer line**: the last line of every document. It is the same setting as on **Hospital letterhead**.

### Receipt printer

If the desk has a small roll-paper receipt printer, choose its paper width in **Receipt printer**: **80 mm roll** or **58 mm roll**. Receipts and queue tickets then get a print button for that printer. Choose **None (A4 only)** if you print only on ordinary A4 paper.

If you are not sure which you have, measure the width of the paper roll.

Click **Save billing settings** when you are done. For printing receipts, see [Billing and payments](07-billing-and-payments.md).

## Messages to patients

**Settings**, then **Messages to patients**. True-Doctor can send short text messages to patients: when an appointment is booked, a reminder the day before, a receipt, a dental check-up reminder, and documents you send by link.

A patient only gets messages if they said yes at the desk. See [Patients](03-patients.md) for how to record their answer.

If the page says **Text messages are not switched on for this server yet**, click **Ask us on WhatsApp**.

### Choosing what patients receive

1. Under **What patients receive**, tick **Send text messages to patients** who agreed to them.
2. Tick each message you want to send: **Appointment booked**, **The day before an appointment**, **Receipt when a visit is paid**, **Dental check-up due**, **A review or check-up is due** (sent only when somebody presses **Text** on **Recalls and reviews due**) and **A document sent by link**. Under each one you can read what the patient will receive.
3. Set the quiet hours with **Quiet from** and **Quiet until**. Nothing is sent between these hours. Messages wait until morning.
4. Click **Save**.

### Checking what was sent

The top of the page shows **Sent this month** and **Failed or not sent**. On the hosted service it also shows **Messages left**. One message is one text of up to 160 letters. When it runs low, click **Buy messages on WhatsApp**.

The table below lists every message with its **Status**. Use the list at the top to show only one kind, for example failed messages. When a message was not sent, the reason is shown under its status, for example:

- **The patient has not agreed to texts**
- **No mobile number on the record**
- **No messages left**
- **This message is switched off**

This is the first place to look when a patient says they did not get a reminder.

The owner can also turn on **My daily summary** on this page. See [Reports and Ministry forms](12-reports-and-ministry-forms.md).
